Sensing of metabolistes inside a nanopore

My first internship I did at biochem 1 (Maglia labratory) at the rijksuniversiteit Groningen under MSc van den Noort

Here, I continued work on a biosensing project within the Maglia group, which focuses on integrating proteins into nanopores to detect ligand binding. Metabolites — small molecules produced by metabolism — are an important class of biomarkers, and their binding can be observed through changes in the ionic current as they pass through the pore. This approach allows metabolites to be identified directly from picoliter volumes of blood, sweat, and other biological fluids.

Within this project, I learned about nanopore technology. I also carried out protein expression and purification using E. coli. The data analysis of the electrophysiology data was performed independently in R. As an extra I also created a python script so I could analyse my data at home.
